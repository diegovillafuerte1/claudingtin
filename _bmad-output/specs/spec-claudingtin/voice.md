# Voice & Tone — Claude Think-Time Chat Roulette

The emotional core — a roulette *spin*, serendipity, a light warm moment between two people — has to survive contact with the requirements. These rules give it teeth. They bind all user-facing copy in the companion and the opener set in the repo.

## Overall voice

- Warm, light, and unpolished-in-a-human-way. Never corporate, never clinical, never therapized. It should feel like a friend nudged you, not like an app onboarded you.
- Short sentences. Lowercase-friendly. No exclamation-mark pile-ups, no emoji required.
- Never name the machinery. The user is "waiting," not "in the FIFO queue"; they "left," they were not "disconnected from the relay."

## Exit and disconnect copy

- Reads as an easy, cheerful "catch you later." The "my Claude came back" fiction carries no apology, no guilt, no "are you sure?".
- Identical for every ending — peer left, block, report, network drop, model returned. No copy anywhere tells a user they were left, rejected, blocked, or reported (CAP-4).
- No alarm language: no "connection lost," no "user is typing…" that lingers, no "they left the chat."

## The spin

- The searching → matched transition gets a small moment of delight — the "spin" landing — as a brief animation or flourish in the pane. It must not become a minigame or cost perceptible time. If the queue resolves, any waiting-state flourish disappears immediately.

## Idle nudges

- While waiting in a slow or empty queue, at most one lightweight nudge at a time — e.g. "while you wait, why not add a line to your profile?" or a link to the community guidelines. Gentle tooltips only.
- Never games, activities, streaks, points, or anything that rewards staying.

## First-run and safety copy

- The first-run safety warning is honest and plain: you are being connected to strangers; don't share identifying, location, or financial information; screenshots and quoting are possible. Warm delivery, but it does not soften the risk.

## Openers

- The curated opener set lives in the open-source repo so contributors can extend it (CAP-7).
- Playful and disarming, skewed toward low-stakes human curiosity. Never survey-like or interview-like. ("What's the last thing that made you laugh?" — yes. "What are your hobbies?" — no.)
- The repo ships this file's Openers section as the one-paragraph voice guide contributed openers are checked against.
